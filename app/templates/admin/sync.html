Sync from Cloudinary

Finds photos already uploaded to the photo-site folder in Cloudinary that aren't in the site's database yet, and adds them using their embedded EXIF/IPTC/XMP metadata (title, caption, camera, lens, date taken, keywords). They're published immediately, same as photos uploaded through the upload form.
